Marsad Connect

This service lets websites running Marsad, a free statistics plugin for WordPress, connect a Google account with one click. It only passes sign-ins through; it keeps no tokens and no statistics.

To use it, install Marsad on your WordPress site and press "Connect with Google" under Marsad → Connections.
